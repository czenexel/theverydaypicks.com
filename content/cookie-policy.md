## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies, such as pixels, local storage, and scripts, work in a comparable way. They allow a website to recognise your device, remember information about your visit, and measure how the site is used.

## Cookies We Use

**Strictly necessary.** Some cookies and technologies are needed for the Website to work and to be delivered securely, for example by our hosting and content delivery provider, Cloudflare.

**Analytics.** We use our own tracking system to understand how many people visit the Website, which pages they read, and how they got here. This helps us improve our content.

**Click and conversion tracking.** When you click a link to a retailer or manufacturer, our tracking system and our partners' systems may set cookies or use similar identifiers. These record that the visit came from our Website so that a resulting purchase can be attributed to us and the commission can be settled. This does not affect the price you pay.

## Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties, such as our advertising partners. These third parties process information under their own privacy policies. We do not control these cookies.

## How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:

- see which cookies are stored and delete them individually or all at once;
- block third-party cookies;
- block all cookies from specific websites or from all websites;
- delete all cookies when you close the browser.

If you block cookies, the Website will still be readable, but some features may not work as intended.
