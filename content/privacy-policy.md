*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how theverydaypicks.com (the "Website") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information about its visitors ("you"). The Website is owned and operated from the United Arab Emirates ("we", "us", or "our").

## Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The operator of theverydaypicks.com is responsible for the processing of personal data on this Website. You can reach us at support@theverydaypicks.com.

## What Information We Collect

**Information you give us.** If you contact us by e-mail, we receive your e-mail address, your name if you provide it, and the content of your message.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Website, our servers and service providers automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, language settings, the page you came from, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit.

**Click and conversion information.** Many of our articles link to the websites of retailers and manufacturers. When you click such a link, we record that click (for example, which article and link you clicked and when). If you later make a purchase on the partner's website, the partner may tell us that a purchase took place so that it can be attributed to our Website. We do not receive your payment details.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- operate, maintain, and secure the Website;
- understand how visitors use the Website and improve our content;
- measure the performance of our articles and links and settle commissions with our partners;
- respond to your questions and requests;
- comply with our legal obligations.

## Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), where we ask for it, for example for non-essential cookies;
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running a secure, functional Website, understanding how it is used, and measuring the results of our partnerships;
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and link tracking. For details and information on how to control them, please see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## Service Providers We Use

We share information with service providers who help us run the Website, only to the extent they need it to provide their services:

- **Hosting and content delivery** – our Website and images are delivered through Cloudflare.
- **Analytics** – we use our own tracking system to measure visits to our articles and understand how the Website is used.
- **Fonts** – the Website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
- **Click and conversion tracking** – we use our own tracking system and those of our advertising partners to record clicks on partner links and attribute resulting sales.

Some of these providers are located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Apart from the service providers listed above, we may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of our business.

## Third-Party Websites

Our articles contain links to websites operated by third parties, such as retailers and manufacturers. When you leave our Website, the privacy policy of that other website applies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites. If you place an order on a partner's website, the partner, not us, is responsible for processing your order data.

## How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. E-mail correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up. Technical and click data is kept for no longer than necessary for analytics, fraud prevention, and settling commissions, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Your Rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict or object to our processing of your data;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **support@theverydaypicks.com**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live.

**California residents** may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us at the address above.

## Children

The Website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. The Website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last changed.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at **support@theverydaypicks.com** or via our [contact page](/contact/).
