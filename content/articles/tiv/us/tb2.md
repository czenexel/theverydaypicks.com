## What most people don't know:

**Problem #1: Every knife "shaves" particles off your board**

With every cut, scratched plastic boards release tiny plastic particles – invisible microplastics that end up in your food. With wood and bamboo boards, it's worn-off lacquers, oils, and protective coatings whose micro-particles also end up on your plate without you realizing it.

**Problem #2: Deep grooves are a breeding ground for germs**

Meat juices, vegetable juices, and moisture collect in the fine cut marks. Bacteria colonies form there and survive even after rinsing – perfectly protected inside the grooves.

**Problem #3: Dishwashers actually make it worse**

Hot water and detergent roughen plastic and bamboo boards – the surface becomes porous, even more particles break loose, and germs find more places to cling. At the same time, most dishwasher cycles don't kill all bacteria – so the board gets rougher and even less hygienic.

**Problem #4: Wood and bamboo aren't automatically "healthy"**

Natural boards absorb 5x more moisture, blood, and fat! This can cause mold or odor to develop on the inside that isn't visible from the outside. This is especially critical with raw meat.

**Problem #5: It all ends up where it's most dangerous – in your food**

What comes off the board doesn't stay on the countertop – it goes directly into what you're cutting: fruit for kids, snacks, vegetables, meat. Babies and toddlers with their sensitive systems are especially at risk.

Many people struggle with recurring digestive issues, inflammation, or allergies without ever suspecting that their old cutting board could be contributing to the problem.
