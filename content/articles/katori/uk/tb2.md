## What most people don't know:

**Problem No. 1: Every knife "shaves" particles off your board**

With every cut, tiny plastic particles detach from scratched plastic boards – invisible microplastics that end up in your food. With wooden and bamboo boards, it is the rubbed-off varnishes, oils, and protective coatings whose microparticles also land unnoticed on your plate.

**Problem No. 2: Deep grooves are a paradise for germs**

Meat juice, vegetable juice, and moisture accumulate in the fine cut marks. Nests of bacteria form there, which survive even after washing – perfectly protected inside the grooves.

**Problem No. 3: Dishwashers make it worse**

Hot water and detergents roughen up plastic and bamboo boards – the surface becomes porous, even more particles come loose, and germs find more grip. At the same time, most dishwasher programmes do not kill all the bacteria – so the board becomes rougher and thus even more unhygienic.

**Problem No. 4: Wood and bamboo aren't automatically "healthy"**

Natural boards absorb 5 times more moisture, blood, and fat! This can cause mould or odours to form inside, which are not visible from the outside. This is particularly critical with raw meat.

**Problem No. 5: Everything ends up where it is most dangerous – in your food**

What comes off the board doesn't stay on the worktop; it goes directly into what you are chopping: fruit for children, snacks, vegetables, meat. Babies and toddlers with their sensitive organisms are particularly affected.

Many people struggle with recurring gastrointestinal complaints, inflammation, or allergies without realising that their old chopping board could be contributing to the cause.
