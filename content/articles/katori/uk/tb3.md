In February 2025, researchers at the University of New Mexico published a study in Nature Medicine – one of the most respected medical journals in the world. They examined the brains of deceased people and found something that made headlines everywhere from the BBC to the Guardian.

**They found microplastics inside human brain tissue. Roughly a spoonful of it.**

The plastic had crossed the blood-brain barrier – the body's most heavily guarded defence system – and accumulated in the brain. The concentration had increased by 50% compared to samples from 2016. It's getting worse. Fast.

And here's the part that hit me personally: **the primary plastic they found was polyethylene.** The exact same material most kitchen chopping boards are made from.

## The Numbers That Kept Me Up at Night

Over the next few days I went down a research rabbit hole I wish I hadn't. But now that I know, I can't pretend I don't. And neither should you.

**A single plastic chopping board releases 7.4 to 50.7 grams of microplastics per person per year.** That's not a typo. Up to 50 grams of plastic dust, shaved off by your kitchen knife, mixed invisibly into every meal you prepare. Every salad. Every piece of chicken. Every piece of fruit you slice for your kids.

**A single knife stroke releases 100 to 300 microplastic particles.** Think about how many cuts you make in a single dinner. Now multiply that by 365 days. Now multiply that by the years you've owned your current board.

The number is in the tens of millions. And every single one of those particles ended up in someone's mouth.

**But it doesn't stay in your stomach.** That's what the research showed. These particles are so small they pass through your intestinal wall and enter your bloodstream. From there, they travel everywhere. Researchers have now found microplastics in human blood, lungs, liver, kidneys, placentas, breast milk, and brain tissue.

You are not just eating plastic. Researchers believe these particles may be accumulating in the body. And the amounts detected appear to be growing year over year.

## What Microplastics Are Doing Inside Your Body

I want to be careful here because I'm not a doctor. But the published studies speak for themselves.

**Heart attacks and strokes.** A 2024 study published in the New England Journal of Medicine examined arterial plaque from 257 patients. 58% had microplastics embedded in their clogged arteries. Patients with plastic in their plaque had a **significantly higher rate of cardiovascular events** over the next three years. The correlation was strong enough to make headlines worldwide.

**Brain accumulation.** The Nature Medicine study found microplastics crossing the blood-brain barrier and building up in brain tissue. Notably, even higher concentrations were observed in certain neurological cases. The researchers stressed that more study is needed – but the fact that it's getting into the brain at all was enough to concern them.

**Babies and children.** Multiple studies have now detected microplastics in human placentas, with higher concentrations observed in certain complicated pregnancies. Children's daily exposure is estimated to be significantly higher per kilogramme of body weight than adults, simply because of their smaller size. All 30 infant formula brands tested in one European study contained microplastic contamination.

**Fertility.** Early animal research has raised concerns about potential effects on reproductive health from long-term microplastic exposure. Microplastics have also been detected in human reproductive tissue.

I read all of this in one sitting. Then I went into my kitchen and looked at that plastic chopping board again.

I grabbed a hand saw from the garage and cut it in half.
