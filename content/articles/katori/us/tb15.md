## What Most People Don't Know

**Problem #1: Every knife "shaves" particles off your board**
With every cut, tiny plastic particles break off scratched plastic boards – invisible microplastics that end up in your food. With wood and bamboo boards, it's worn-off coatings, oils, and protective finishes whose micro-particles also end up on your plate without you noticing.

**Problem #2: Deep grooves are a paradise for germs**
Meat juice, vegetable juice, and moisture collect inside those fine cut marks. That's where bacteria nests form – and they survive even a thorough rinse, perfectly protected inside the grooves.

**Problem #3: Dishwashers make it worse**
Hot water and detergents roughen up plastic and bamboo boards – the surface becomes porous, even more particles break loose, and germs get a better foothold. At the same time, most dishwasher cycles don't kill all the bacteria – so your board ends up rougher and even less hygienic than before.

**Problem #4: Wood & bamboo aren't automatically "healthy"**
Natural boards absorb moisture, blood, and fat 5 times more! That leads to mold or odors forming inside the board where you can't see them. Especially concerning with raw meat.

**Problem #5: Everything ends up where it's most dangerous – in your food**
Whatever breaks off the board doesn't just stay on the counter – it goes straight into whatever you're cutting: fruit for your kids, sandwiches, vegetables, meat. Babies and toddlers, with their sensitive systems, are exposed to the most of it.

Many people struggle with recurring stomach issues, inflammation, or allergies – without any idea that their trusty old cutting board could be partly to blame.
