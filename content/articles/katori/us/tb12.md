Most cutting boards fall into one of three categories. And every single one of them has a massive problem.

**Plastic boards** are the most common – and the most dangerous. Every time a knife slides across the surface, it cuts a tiny groove into the plastic. Those furrows are invisible to the naked eye at first, but they add up shockingly fast. Within a few weeks, your board is covered with thousands of micro-cuts. Bacteria nestle into them, multiply, and no amount of thorough washing can ever fully remove them. Worse still: those very same cuts release microplastic particles directly into your food.

**Wooden boards** seem like the natural alternative, but they have their own hidden problem. Wood is porous. It absorbs moisture, meat juices, and odors deep into its fibers. That's why your wooden board still smells like onions three days later. That trapped moisture creates the perfect breeding ground for mold and bacteria beneath the surface, completely invisible and impossible to clean. Plus, they require constant oiling and maintenance, or they crack and warp.

**Glass and ceramic boards** solve the hygiene problem but create a new one: they destroy your knives. The surface is so hard that it chips and dulls the blade with every cut. Within days, your knives are blunt. And a dull knife is far more dangerous than a sharp one – because you're forced to press harder, and that's exactly when blades slip.

That's the trap most home cooks are stuck in. You're either feeding your family bacteria and plastic, ruining your knives, or both.
