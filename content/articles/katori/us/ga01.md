## What Is Katori, And Why Is It Rated So High?

Katori is a cutting board made from **solid titanium** — an inert, food-safe metal best known for being used in medical implants because the body doesn't react to it. On a cutting board, that same property matters for a different reason: nothing leaches, flakes, or sheds into your food.

That's a bigger deal than most home cooks realise. Recent research has shown that ordinary plastic boards shed enormous numbers of microplastic particles into food, and that scratched-up boards of any porous material become a hiding place for bacteria that survive normal washing. Titanium sidesteps both problems at once — it's non-porous and it doesn't break down.

After weeks of daily use, it's easy to see why Katori keeps landing at the top of "best cutting board" lists: it solves the hygiene problem without the trade-offs that come with wood, bamboo, glass, or plastic.
