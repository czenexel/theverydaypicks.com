## What most people don't know:

**Problem #1: Every knife "shaves" particles off your board**

With every cut, scratched plastic boards release tiny plastic particles – invisible microplastics that end up in your food. With wood and bamboo boards, it's abraded lacquers, oils, and protective coatings, whose microparticles also end up on your plate without you noticing.

**Problem #2: Deep grooves are a paradise for germs**

Meat juices, vegetable juices, and moisture collect in the fine cut marks. Bacterial colonies form there that survive even after rinsing – perfectly protected inside the grooves.

**Problem #3: Dishwashers make it worse**

Hot water and detergent roughen plastic and bamboo boards – the surface becomes porous, even more particles break loose, and germs find more places to cling. At the same time, most dishwasher cycles don't kill all bacteria – so the board gets rougher and even less hygienic.

**Problem #4: Wood and bamboo aren't automatically "healthy"**

Natural boards absorb 5 times more moisture, blood, and fat! This can lead to mould or odours forming inside that aren't visible from the outside. This is especially critical when handling raw meat.

**Problem #5: It all ends up where it's most dangerous – in your food**

What comes off the board doesn't stay on the countertop – it goes directly into what you're cutting: fruit for your kids, snacks, vegetables, meat. Babies and toddlers with their sensitive systems are especially at risk.

Many people struggle with recurring digestive issues, inflammation, or allergies without ever suspecting that their old cutting board could be a contributing cause.
