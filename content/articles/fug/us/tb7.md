{{< epic-h2 >}}A foam, not a liquid (and not a plumber){{< /epic-h2 >}}

Fuugu is a powder you pour into the drain or toilet and activate with warm water. Within seconds it turns into a thick foam that expands in every direction and clings to the pipe walls — which is where the actual buildup sits.

**It's not a liquid drain cleaner.** Liquids run straight down the middle of the pipe and barely touch the walls. This foam fills the pipe and stays in contact with the grime long enough to break it down.

**It's not a plumber.** It won't blast open a pipe that's already blocked solid in an emergency. It's built to dissolve the gradual buildup — biofilm, grease, soap scum — that makes drains slow and smelly in the first place. If those two things are clear to you, the rest of this page will be worth your time.
